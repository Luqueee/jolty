# Milestone 13 PEFT experiment protocol

Status: the v9 corpus, fitting recipe, and model-selection rule were fixed before fitting. The matched frozen head and LoRA candidate have been fitted on train and calibrated on validation. No v9 test prediction has been scored, and no runtime provider exists yet.

## Question and scope

Test whether adapting the pinned `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` encoder with LoRA improves Jolty's next-action decisions over a frozen encoder trained on the **same** new corpus. The v8 head beat Laya on ten new one-step cases but tied the fixed v7 head on those observations. It also repeated the catalog search-field error in all three measured multistep runs and the historical `learn-create-shadow` error at confidence 0.998746. More easy one-step labels cannot answer whether encoder adaptation fixes these problems.

This experiment is limited to `click`, `type`, and native `select` decisions whose targets appear in the main-document Top-10. Shadow-root and frame targets, drag-and-drop, and target-free actions require separate browser-state or action contracts; they must be recorded as retrieval or capability failures, not treated as encoder errors. The small model remains outside Jolty's normal browser path until a later deployment decision.

## Site and data allocation

The existing v8 training and calibration origins retain their splits. All test origins from v0–v8 remain reserved. The six new origins below were smoke-checked with Chromium on 2026-09-24; a successful page load is not a validated decision label.

| Split | New origins | Intended hard cases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Train | [Play QA](https://www.play-qa.com/), [UPEX Dojo](https://dojo.upexgalaxy.com/) | Product and autocomplete search fields beside action buttons; repeated form fields; dialog close versus reopen; custom and native controls; table actions after a state change. |
| Calibration | [Syntax](https://www.syntaxprojects.com/) | Ambiguous labels, form and widget choices, and changed page state. Use only main-document controls. |
| Untouched test | [Automation Exercise](https://automationexercise.com/), [DemoBlaze](https://www.demoblaze.com/), [ParaBank](https://parabank.parasoft.com/parabank/index.htm) | Search and product navigation, cart or modal choices, and banking-form navigation without creating accounts or making transactions. |

`sources.ts` reserves these origins now. Do not move any evaluated test origin into train or calibration, including the v7/v8 sites and the two inspected multistep sites. Avoid sibling deployments of the same application across splits. If a new site becomes unusable before fitting, replace its **entire origin** and record the replacement and reason before scoring any test prediction.

GlobalSQA was replaced before label authoring or model scoring on 2026-09-24: its practice widgets are embedded in frames, outside this experiment's main-document target contract. Syntax offers main-document fields, tables, filters, and native selects. One Chromium observation found 1,103 extracted elements and 80.24 ms extraction on Automation Exercise's product page. This is a single cold observation, not a latency distribution. Report extraction separately from decision latency on this site; do not attribute total-loop time to the model. Its size is a useful stress case for the existing compact-state contract and may require a separately measured browser-state change before deployment.

The next corpus must contain at least 60 **new**, independently validated hard training decisions across the two new training origins, with at least 25 from each, at least 20 new calibration decisions on Syntax, and at least 30 distinct held-out decision states across the three new test origins, with at least ten per test origin. Author at least three multistep flows of three or more steps per test origin. Flow steps may contribute to the 30 decision states, but repeated runs and paraphrases of one state never increase the distinct-label count. Include at least six cases each for search-field/button conflict, duplicate or nearby labels, and post-transition controls across train and calibration. Keep click, type, and select represented in train and calibration; test action coverage depends on what the sites support. Do not manufacture an action merely to satisfy a count.

Each label needs a stable starting state, exact target and action, planned value when applicable, target Recall@10, executor success, deterministic validator result, and an independent postcondition. Run each newly authored flow's curated reference in three fresh browser contexts before admitting it. A source page that changes, requires credentials, or fails the privacy guard is excluded or repaired before any model score is read. Save a corpus digest, split groups, action counts, failure-mode tags, and the exact case list. Never promote a previously inspected test result into fitting or threshold selection.

## Fixed model comparison

Both candidates use the same validated training observations and Top-10 options. First fit the existing 388-feature frozen head on the new train split with the v8 zero-action-intercept recipe; use the new calibration split for its temperature and threshold. This is the matched baseline. Then adapt the **same pinned encoder revision** with LoRA on attention `query` and `value` projections (rank 8, alpha 16, dropout 0.05), mean pooling and unit normalization, and the same listwise candidate scoring contract. Initialize the 388-feature head from the matched frozen baseline and train the adapter and head together with listwise cross-entropy, keeping the three global action-intercept weights fixed at zero. Use maximum sequence length 128, four epochs, AdamW learning rate `2e-4`, weight decay 0.01, one decision group per microbatch, eight gradient-accumulation steps, and a fixed seed of 17. Do not tune these values on test results. Before fitting, compare the Python and TypeScript feature construction on 20 saved training/calibration observations: action options and top choices must match exactly, and each normalized embedding pair must have cosine similarity at least 0.999. Stop on a parity failure.

Training belongs in a separate Python process launched by `pnpm research`; no Python runtime enters `packages/browser`, `packages/decision`, or the normal CLI execution path. Use the local 8 GB NVIDIA GPU only after a one-batch memory smoke test. Record peak VRAM, elapsed training time, dependency versions, model revision, corpus digest, optimizer settings, adapter weights, and head weights. The repository currently has no Python training environment; add the minimal pinned training dependencies only when the new corpus passes admission. The official [PEFT LoRA configuration](https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/en/package_reference/lora) supports explicit projection targets and rank, while [PyTorch AMP](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/accelerator/amp.html) provides mixed-precision training if the memory smoke test requires it.

Choose the calibration temperature and abstention threshold without test labels. Score the untouched test once after the corpus, fitting recipe, and model-selection rule are frozen. Run the matched frozen head, PEFT candidate, Laya, and subscription-backed `gpt-6-sol` teacher on the same saved decision states; run the executable policies on the same live flows. The teacher scores saved observations only. Report per-origin and per-failure-mode exact action-and-target accuracy, task completion, target Recall@10, covered correct/incorrect decisions, confidence buckets and Brier score, p50/p95 decision latency, peak RSS/VRAM, and throughput. Distinguish model errors from retrieval, execution, validation, and site failures.

The engineering promotion gate requires PEFT to gain at least five exact decisions among the 30 held-out states **and** two completed flows among the nine minimum flows over the matched frozen baseline, exceed Laya in held-out task completion, and make no wrong decision at confidence 0.9 or above. It must also select the catalog search field correctly and either correct the historical `learn-create-shadow` decision or abstain below the calibration threshold; these historical cases are regression checks, not part of the untouched gain count. A passing result still needs ONNX export and measured Node inference before runtime integration. If the gate fails, record the negative result and stop rather than changing the adapter or case list after seeing test outcomes. Thirty states on three sites are an engineering gate, not a population-level accuracy claim.

## Fitting run before held-out scoring

Use `pnpm research peft --stage setup` to create the ignored Python environment and install the pinned top-level dependencies in `research/peft/requirements.txt`. Run `pnpm research train --version 9 --bias zero` for the matched frozen head, then `pnpm research peft --stage parity`, `pnpm research peft --stage smoke`, and `pnpm research peft --stage train`. The last three commands verify the same 20 saved train/validation states against the pinned TypeScript encoder before touching the GPU. The training input contains only train and validation samples; both the frozen-head report and PEFT report omit the test split. Python remains in a separate research process and its artifacts are ignored under `artifacts/`.

The 2026-09-24 fitting run used corpus digest `067e957e44c7d33e39f03527da2b754323ff31429d52e164921b08e9ad40914c`. The existing action contract excluded three UPEX custom-select training labels from **both** candidates; 168 training and 76 validation states entered fitting and calibration. No validation or test label was excluded. Parity compared 96 normalized embeddings across 20 states spanning both new training origins and Syntax: minimum cosine 0.99999988, with identical action options and top choices. The one-batch GPU smoke allocated at most 122,979,840 bytes on an 8 GB RTX 4060 Ti. Four LoRA epochs took 3.33 seconds and peaked at 165,925,888 allocated GPU bytes. These are PyTorch allocation measurements, not total device occupancy or inference benchmarks.
